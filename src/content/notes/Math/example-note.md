## Writing maths

Put maths between double dollar signs. Inside a sentence it stays inline, like $$E = mc^2$$ or $$\bar{x} = \frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^{n} x_i$$.

Put the double dollar signs on their own lines for a centred equation:

$$
\text{profit} = \sum_{t=1}^{T} p_t \,(d_t - c_t)
$$

A single dollar sign is just a dollar sign, so prices like $45/MWh are safe to write.
